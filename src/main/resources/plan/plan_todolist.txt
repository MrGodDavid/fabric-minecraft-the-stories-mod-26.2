#TITLE Minecraft: The Stories Mod

#GAMEPLAY
	->
		@LINK https://app.diagrams.net/#HMrGodDavid%2Ffabric-minecraft-the-stories-mod-26.2%2Fmain%2F.github%2FMTSModPlanning.drawio#%7B%22pageId%22%3A%22xBtbFhSAIO169gTzjdz9%22%7D
		Player is the adventurer who explores an abandoned server; the time is about a thousand years after the Last Event in MTS fiction timeline.
		Player's goal is to explore the forgotten world, explore the back stories, and defeat bosses who are still corrupting the world.
		Player needs to defeat nine ancient spirits of the forest and collect [SOMETHING] to unlock the gate of the Crystalized Cave.
	->
	
#FEATURE
		@BIOMES
			-> 
				@BIOME CRYSTALIZED_CAVE: A corrupted cave under the influence of Seal Of Herobrine.
				@BIOME DENSE_WOOD: Ancient forests that are controlled by ancient spirits. The rise of ancient spirits occurred after the extinction of the species of NPC PLAYER & PLAYER.
				@BIOME DEEP_OCEANIA: The place where hosted by the Kingdom of Ocean.
				
				@BIOME WILD_ACACIA: Home of the large Acacia trees and other special Acacian entities.
				@BIOME GIGANTIC_BIRCHLIN: Host of the Gigantic Birch trees. Watch for steps while climbing the trees!
				@BIOME BLOOMY_CHERRY_WOODS: Denser and bigger cherry trees and dark cherry trees.
				@BIOME DARK_OAKLIN: Hoster Dense Dark Oak Trees.
				@BIOME LUSH_JUNGLE: Jungle trees are tall, but Jungles in Lush Jungle is EVEN taller!
				@BIOME SWAMPY_MANGRILLIN: Dangerous swamp that took several life of previous adventurers. 
				@BIOME DENSE_OAKLIN: Dense oak trees are growing in this biome.
				@BIOME PALE_OAKLIN: The Dense oak biome in the version of Pale Garden.
				@BIOME SPRUCIA: Taiga biome is huge, Sprucia is denser and larger than Taiga.
			-> 
		@BOSSES
			->
				@ENTITY HEROBRINE: Final boss of the Overworld.
				@ENTITY FUEGOLA: Final boss of the Nether.
				@ENTITY VOIDER: Final boss of the End.
			->
		@ENTITIES
			->
				@ENTITY BROWN_BEAR: Giant Brown Bear that live in forests. They would not attack players unless players hold honey blocks or honey bottles. They are NEUTRAL_MOBS. They can be tamed with salmons. Player can use saddle
					to ride brown bears. Brown bears would attack bees, pigs, sheep, cows, and chicken, foxes, and wolfs. (@WIP)
				@ENTITY DEER: Deer has male and female variant. Male can only breed with female. Deer is ridable and tamable animal.
				@ENTITY DUMMY_TARGET: Target dummy. Like Armor Stands. Inspired by an awesome youtube video, and I will link his channel and video in here. Dummy Target displays the attack point that player dealt.
					@BUG Target dummy would  be killed when player smashes target dummy with mace.
				@ENTITY ACACIANIA: Spirit of the Wild Acacia. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants Anti-dehydration effects to all surrounding entities when it is tamed by player.
				@ENTITY LOUDER: Entity in 
				@ENTITY BIRCHIA: Spirit of Gigantic Birchlin. Tamable auxiliary entity.
				@ENTITY CHERRINA: Spirit of Bloomy Cherry Woods. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants Entice effect--decrease damage dealt by surrounding enemies.
				@ENTITY DENSE_OAKIE: Spirit of Dark Oaklin. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants Strength effects. Increase damage dealt by player in the range of this dense_oakie.
				@ENTITY JUNGLENIA: Spirit of Lush Jungle. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants Swift effects. Increase player's speed in the range of junglenia.
				@ENTITY MANGROVINA: Spirit of Swampy Mangrilin. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants Swimmer and Hydration buff in range of mangrovia. 
				@ENTITY OAKIE: Spirit of the Dense Oaklin. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants healing effects to all surrounding entity, including the time when it is tamed by player. (@WIP)
				@ENTITY LIGHT_OAKIE: Spirit of Pale Oaklin. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants Jumping effect, increasing player's jumping height in the range of Light Oakie.
				@ENTITY SPRUCIA: Spirit of Sprucia. Tamable auxiliary entity. Grants Attacker effect, increasing attack speed and damage on player.
			->
		@STRUCTURES

				@STRUCTURE MAZE_OF_HEROBRINE: The place where held the Seal of Herobrine.
