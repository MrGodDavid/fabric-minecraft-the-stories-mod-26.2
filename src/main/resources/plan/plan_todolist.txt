#TITLE Minecraft: The Stories Mod

#GAMEPLAY
	->
		@LINK https://app.diagrams.net/#HMrGodDavid%2Ffabric-minecraft-the-stories-mod-26.2%2Fmain%2F.github%2FMTSModPlanning.drawio#%7B%22pageId%22%3A%22xBtbFhSAIO169gTzjdz9%22%7D
		Player is the adventurer who explores an abandoned server; the time is about a thousand years after the Last Event in MTS fiction timeline.
		Player's goal is to explore the forgotten world, explore the back stories, and defeat bosses who are still corrupting the world.
		Player needs to defeat nine ancient spirits of the forest and collect [SOMETHING] to unlock the gate of the Crystalized Cave.
	->
	
#FEATURE
		@BIOMES
			-> 
				@BIOME CRYSTALIZED_CAVE: A corrupted cave under the influence of Seal Of Herobrine.
				@BIOME DENSE_WOOD: Ancient forests that are controlled by ancient spirits. The rise of ancient spirits occurred after the extinction of the species of NPC PLAYER & PLAYER.
				@BIOME DEEP_OCEANIA: The place where hosted by the Kingdom of Ocean.
			-> 
		@BOSSES
			->
				@ENTITY HEROBRINE: Final boss of the Overworld.
				@ENTITY FUEGOLA: Final boss of the Nether.
				@ENTITY VOIDER: Final boss of the End.
			->
		@ENTITIES
			->
				@ENTITY BROWN_BEAR: Giant Brown Bear that live in forests. They would not attack players unless players hold honey blocks or honey bottles. They are NEUTRAL_MOBS. They can be tamed with salmons. Player can use saddle
					to ride brown bears. Brown bears would attack bees, pigs, sheep, cows, and chicken, foxes, and wolfs. (@WIP)
				@ENTITY DEER: Deer has male and female variant. Male can only breed with female. Deer is ridable and tamable animal.
				@ENTITY DUMMY_TARGET: Target dummy. Like Armor Stands. Inspired by an awesome youtube video, and I will link his channel and video in here. Dummy Target displays the attack point that player dealt.
					@BUG Target dummy will be killed if player smashes target dummy with mace.
			->
		@STRUCTURES
			->
				@STRUCTURE MAZE_OF_HEROBRINE: The place where held the Seal of Herobrine.
				@STRUCTURE SEAL_OF_HEROBRINE: The core where Herobrine being sealed.
				@STRUCTURE RUINED_VILLAGER_OUTPOST: Ancient warfare structure used for watching the border between Kingdom of Villagers and Pillager Empire.
			->
