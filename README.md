# Description/Background

Minecraft: The Stories is a developing mod that tells the past forgotten stories in the Western Land. The entire story plot is based on my first personal Minecraft comic. (I never published the entire comic on any of my social media platforms. You can find the first chapter of my comic on my YouTube. Probably only me the person who can understand it now, lol.) Note: The following information of description/background might confuses you that this mod supports Multiplayers. This mod DOES NOT support multiplayers. 

Western Land is a geographic location in the Overworld. Civilizations and kingdoms raised and fell on this land, but only the fews, who feels carrying responsibilities, recorded the history of the past. These books scattered across every corners of Western Land, hiding itself in the shadow, waiting for our protagonist.

The protagonist, or our adventurer, is a newly-joined player in the world. Like any other newly-joined players, our adventurer needs to gain resources while avoiding huntings/raiding of other hostile hybrid players/NPCs. Our adventurer needs to build a base. The location of the base can be anyplace, from absolutely wildness to near villages/cities. Adventurer is allowed freely traveling to different kingdoms. Adventurer will encounter different tasks and riddles, which allows our adventurer to explore the unknown, discover the forgotten stories, and find more power weapons.

This mod changed some of the playing features of vanilla Minecraft, which might make you feel surprised or frustrated. This is my first time of doing mod development. I cannot guarantee this can become a playable cool mod. I hope I can solve every problems that I'm gonna encounter.
